Questions

Will it make up prices or promise things I don't do?

No. It only uses the info you approve. If it doesn't know, it takes the customer's details and passes the lead to you.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Missed-call text-back works with your existing mobile or business number via call forwarding.

What about customer privacy?

Customer data is used only to handle enquiries for your business. We don't sell data, and marketing messages are only sent with consent.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month.
